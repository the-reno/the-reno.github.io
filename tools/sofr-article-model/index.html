How this decision is calculated
  1. Add six calendar months to the decision date. Move to the next observed business day unless it falls in the next month, then use the previous one.
  2. Calculate locked interest as the starting 6M quote × actual days ÷ 360.
  3. For each ON observation, use 1 + rate × calendar days to the next observation ÷ 360. Multiply the factors through maturity and subtract 1.
  4. Subtract the compounded ON return from the locked return and multiply by 10,000 for holding-period basis points.
