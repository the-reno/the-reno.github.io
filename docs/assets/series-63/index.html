03.1

Securities, accounts and investment arrangements

Direct definition

Security. An instrument or arrangement within the governing definition, including stock, bonds and investment contracts.

A share represents ownership; a bond represents a debt claim. An account is the arrangement holding such assets. Putting a bond in an individual retirement account (IRA) does not change the bond into a different kind of instrument.

The full arrangement matters. A exempt from a requirement remains a security; a nonsecurity falls outside the particular definition in the first place.
