01Do now / confirm the frontOne focused survey, no extra general photos
  1. Draw the front as F1 left doors, F2 center post and F3 right bay. Keep these labels on measurements and receipts.
  2. Measure each opening at top, middle and bottom; height at left, middle and right; then both diagonals. Record the smallest clear dimensions and any difference between diagonals.
  3. Record each left leaf’s width, height and thickness, hinge positions, and available bottom clearance throughout its swing. Do not subtract a guessed gap to create a cut list.
  4. Mark the proposed right-side entrance on the floor with removable tape. Check the inward swing leaves an unobstructed access route.
  5. Obtain the exact prehung unit’s frame dimensions, rough opening, sill support, fastener and handing instructions before laying out its framing.
Before disturbing anythingOld coatings and siding have not been tested. Do not sand, scrape, saw or remove unknown painted or fibrous material until the hazard assessment and safe method are settled. No post, beam, brace or header removal is included.
02Approvals, moisture & repair decisionSmall checks prevent expensive rework
  1. Send the before/after front sketch to Westfield Building and Zoning. Confirm permits, drawings, inspections and any exterior-design restrictions before altering the opening.
  2. Identify water entry at the threshold and lower door edges. Record slab cracks and unevenness; a photograph cannot establish their cause or slab anchorage capacity.
  3. After material hazards are addressed, have deteriorated rails/stiles, frame joints, hinges and support wood checked. Surface damage alone does not tell us what is reusable.
  4. Choose repair only where remaining door structure is sound. If load-carrying joints or hinge attachments are compromised, stop the repair allowance and obtain a rebuild/replacement price.
Release to purchaseConfirmed approvals, a suitable dry opening, verified attachment surfaces and a selected door unit. Insulation, electrical and whole-garage finishes are later phases.
03F1 / repair the left paired doorsReuse sound components; avoid extra weight
  1. Label both leaves, hinges and shims. Secure the work area and have help support each heavy glazed leaf before removing any hinge fastener. Use stable padded trestles.
  2. Retain sound timber and glazing. Replace failed components with matching-thickness exterior-suitable material using the reviewed repair/joinery detail; cosmetic filler is not a structural repair.
  3. Keep the frames square while repairing. Prime/finish new cut edges and end grain as specified by the coating system before assembly; follow the approved lead-safe preparation method for old coatings.
  4. Refit with hinges and fasteners rated for the finished leaf weight and actual supporting wood. Do not assume a standard interior-door hinge set is adequate for these approximately 50-in-wide leaves.
  5. Fit a meeting-stile cover/seal, perimeter seals and a suitable bottom seal after verifying clearances. Keep drainage paths open. Reuse sound latches; replace only what fails the check.
  6. Open and latch each leaf repeatedly. Confirm no scraping, binding, loosening or unsealed daylight at closed joints. Do not add rigid insulation or heavy cladding until hardware/weight and moisture details are reviewed.
Ready whenBoth leaves operate without sagging or dragging, hardware is secure, timber is protected and seals make consistent contact. Failed frames need a separate rebuild detail, not this repair sequence.
04F3 / frame the right-bay enclosureNew non-load-bearing infill; existing structure retained
  1. Keep the existing post/header and all roof supports untouched. Secure the opening while working; remove only the agreed right-hand leaves and nonstructural trim after safe material handling is arranged.
  2. Set out the stock door’s actual rough opening toward the right. Leave enough jamb return and attachment space for the reviewed framing detail; nominal 36 × 80 in is not the rough opening.
  3. Build the agreed 2×4 enclosure frame with a treated sole plate and capillary break at concrete. Provisionally allow 16-in-on-center studs, but follow the approved spacing, wind-load attachment and local requirements.
  4. Confirm slab thickness, condition, edges and concealed services before drilling. Use the specified anchor type, embedment, spacing and edge distances—not a generic online anchor pattern. Use fasteners compatible with treated lumber.
  5. Provide jamb backing, top infill and sill support to the selected door instructions. New framing encloses the bay; it must not be treated as a replacement roof-support system.
Ready whenThe frame is plumb, square and securely connected under the reviewed detail; the exact door rough opening and any required inspection are accepted. No final timber cutting schedule until then.
05Waterproof, install the door & finishOne continuous drainage path to the outside

Wall layers, outside → inside: paint-grade exterior siding → manufacturer-required drainage space → water-resistive barrier (WRB) → rated sheathing → 2×4 frame. Interior insulation and lining are deferred.

  1. Install rated sheathing with its specified edge gaps and fastening. Integrate the new WRB into the existing water-control layer; if that cannot be accessed, pause for a connection detail.
  2. Prepare the opening and install its drained sill pan and threshold support according to the selected door/flashing system. Use compatible tapes and sealants; keep the outward drainage path open.
  3. Set the prehung door following its manufacturer’s instructions: required sealant locations, shims, frame fasteners and plumb/square checks. Test the swing and latch before closing the joints.
  4. Complete jamb/head flashing and the drip-cap tie-in in the order specified for the unit and WRB system. Lap upper layers over lower layers so water exits outside; do not block drainage.
  5. Insulate/seal the frame-to-opening gap using the specified backer rod/sealant or low-expansion window-and-door foam. Foam is not structural fastening; do not block pan drainage.
  6. Install cladding and trim with the selected product’s ground/paving clearances and flashing details. Do not run wood-based siding down onto wet asphalt or concrete.
  7. Finish exposed cut edges and surfaces, adjust seals, verify drainage and recheck after rain. Complete the required inspection before concealing work.
Ready whenDoor swings freely and latches without force, weather seals contact evenly, and the wall/threshold shed water outward. Thermal upgrades come after the enclosure is dry.

Measure now: tape, framing square, 4-ft level, pencil, removable tape, notebook. A laser measure is optional; it does not replace opening diagonals.

Borrow first: drill/driver, circular saw with guide, clamps, stable trestles, caulking gun and hand tools. Rent a concrete drill only after the slab/anchor detail is confirmed.
