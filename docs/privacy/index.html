Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Ronu is a public website hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not require an account to read.

Visit records

For each accepted page or article visit, Ronu stores the time, page, connection IP address, country, and first-level region. Country and region are approximate and are supplied by Cloudflare from the incoming network connection.

Ronu does not store city, postal code, latitude or longitude, cookies, account identifiers, or device fingerprints with the visit record. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected.

Feedback

The optional Feedback form can send the page or article, a rating, your comment, and any name or email you choose to provide. Feedback is stored privately and is not posted on the website. Feedback is kept in a separate table and does not store or automatically join the visit IP address.

Email is used for a reply when provided. The separate updates checkbox records a request for occasional updates.

Retention

Visit and feedback records remain until manually deleted. Aggregate page-view totals may remain after individual visit records are deleted. Private CSV exports may also be retained separately after a database row is deleted.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the public site. Cloudflare processes page-view and feedback requests, provides approximate country/region information, and stores the private records. Provider infrastructure logs are separate from Ronu's database.

Visitor records are not sold and are not used for advertising.

Requests and changes

To ask about, correct, or delete information you submitted through Feedback, use the Feedback form and provide a reply email when needed. This policy will be updated when the site's data practices change.
