Privacy & statistics

Effective September 30, 2026

Ronu.one is operated from the United States and hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not require an account to read.

Your browser choices

Google Analytics is optional and off until you enable it here. Enabling it allows Google to receive page views, engagement time, approximate location and device information, and to use analytics cookies. This helps us understand which articles people read.

Your choice applies to this browser. Google Analytics stays off on this Privacy page.

Google Analytics stays off when JavaScript is unavailable.

Cloudflare visit records

For each accepted page or article visit, Ronu stores the time, page, connection IP address, country, and first-level region. Country and region are approximate and are supplied by Cloudflare from the incoming network connection.

Ronu does not store city, postal code, latitude or longitude, cookies, account identifiers, or device fingerprints with the visit record. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected.

The browser also sends active time, maximum scroll depth, page order and randomly generated visit and temporary session identifiers. The session identifier lasts only in the open page's memory and is not stored in a cookie. These records help us understand readership and compare the two measurement systems. Turning Google Analytics off does not turn Cloudflare records off; use the browser exclusion above to stop both.

Optional Google Analytics

Only after you enable it, the homepage, articles and section pages load Google Analytics to measure page views and engagement time. Google also receives device/browser information and uses the connection IP address to estimate country, region and city. These are approximate locations, not GPS readings. Google states that Analytics discards the IP address before logging the data.

We do not send feedback, names, email addresses, search input, URL query strings or fragments to Google. Advertising consent stays denied, and Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in the site's tag. Enhanced measurement for forms, searches, outbound clicks, scrolling and videos is off.

After opt-in, Google uses first-party analytics cookies such as _ga and _ga_… to distinguish browsers and sessions. We limit their lifetime to 180 days without extending it on each visit. The opt-in also expires after 180 days. You can withdraw it here at any time; we then stop Google collection and remove these cookies. Withdrawal does not delete data already received by Google.

The settings above use local browser storage to remember your choices. The browser exclusion lasts until you switch it off or clear the site's storage. It must be set separately on each browser or device. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals override a Google opt-in and stop both visit trackers.

Google processes the optional analytics data on our behalf, including outside your country. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. We rely on your consent for this optional Google measurement.

Feedback

The optional Feedback form can send the page or article, a rating, your comment, and any name or email you choose to provide. Feedback is stored privately and is not posted on the website. Feedback is kept in a separate table and does not store or automatically join the visit IP address.

Email is used for a reply when provided. The separate updates checkbox records a request for occasional updates.

Retention

Visit and feedback records remain until manually deleted. Aggregate page-view totals may remain after individual visit records are deleted. Private CSV exports may also be retained separately after a database row is deleted.

Google's event and user data retention is set to 2 months. Aggregated standard reports can remain available longer. You may withdraw Google consent using the controls above.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the public site. Cloudflare processes page-view and feedback requests, provides approximate country/region information, and stores the private records. Google processes analytics only for browsers that opt in. Provider infrastructure logs are separate from Ronu's database and the browser exclusion does not disable those logs.

Visitor records are not sold and are not used for advertising.

Requests and changes

To ask about, correct, or delete information you submitted through Feedback, use the Feedback form and provide a reply email when needed. This policy will be updated when the site's data practices change.
